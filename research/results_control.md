# Does the cap gain survive removing mean reversion?

Generated by `research/control_362.py`. Synthetic data only, 20 paired seeds, 20000 events, delay 0, no fees. Intervals are approximate 95% t intervals across seeds. `gain` is cap 20 minus cap 5 on the same seed.

Rule fixed before the first run: the gain vanishes if its interval includes zero, is negative, or its mean is below a quarter of the original gain.

### Added after seeing the table

Not part of the fixed rule. The variance-ratio gate could not be met at any step, and it was a poor gate: the denominator is the one-event variance, which bid-ask bounce inflates, and a 5000-event window in a 20000-event path is biased low. So the flow control has no pre-specified verdict. Two facts can still be read, as post hoc observations. First, the gain does not fall steadily with step size: it is noisy, and intervals widen sharply. Second, fair value in these rows is an exact martingale, so inventory held against it earns nothing on average whatever the mid does; this table shows that term directly, next to the gain in the mid-marked position term.

## Mark control

Original stream. Each mid change has its in-sample prediction from distance to trailing means (100, 1000, 5000 events) removed before position P&L is summed; spread capture is unchanged.

| measure | gain |
|---|---|
| P&L as marked | 232.8 [158.5, 307.1] |
| P&L with predictable mid change removed | 140.0 [-1.0, 281.0] |
| position term as marked | 191.2 [117.4, 265.0] |
| position term after removal | 98.4 [-43.3, 240.2] |

Verdict by the rule: **vanishes**. The mean that remains is 60% of the original gain, so read this with the interval, not as a gain measured to be gone.

## Reading

The attribution in `results_sim.md` is not settled by this test. The flow control gave no verdict under its fixed rule. The mark control's linear in-sample prediction removed part of the gain, not all of it. Mean reversion remains a supported explanation of part of the gain; proof that it is the whole cause was not obtained.

Limits: 20 seeds, one maker, one generator family. The mark control's prediction is linear and in sample. Nothing here says anything about real markets.
