# Model extension results

Generated by `python research/run_models.py`; do not edit by hand. Prices are ticks.

**Limits.** Synthetic only: simulated Hawkes paths with known kernels, and the zero-intelligence stream, which has no informed traders and does not react to orders. Nothing here says anything about real markets, and no number below is evidence of a tradable edge. Every Hawkes fit knows the right family is among its candidates; real kernels need not be. Rates and branching are not constrained to stationarity.

## 1. Kernels

Reference for AIC and BIC columns: `exp K=1`. n is the branching ratio. KS and LB (Ljung-Box, 10 lags) are 5% rejection rates on time-rescaling residuals; the KS p-value ignores parameter estimation, so it is conservative. The 95% interval is for the mean over paths, not for one path. `10 fixed rates` counts only non-zero weights as parameters, so its AIC is indicative.
