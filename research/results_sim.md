# Execution simulator results

Generated by `research/run_sim.py`: 40 seeds, 600 s of clock time per run, one lot per quote. P&L is in ticks per run. Brackets are approximate 95% t intervals across seeds. Latency is the one-way delay, applied to market data and to order entry alike.

**Limits.** This is uncalibrated synthetic flow and every parameter is assumed. Levels of P&L mean nothing outside this model. Only signs and orderings inside the model are worth reading, and those depend on the assumptions listed at the end. Nothing here is evidence of a tradable edge.

## Acceptance checks

1. **Latency hurts the maker with informed flow on: PASS.** Naive maker, 2 ms minus 100 ms, paired by seed: 73.2 [52.0, 94.5] ticks. AS maker: 104.5 [78.9, 130.1]. With informed flow off, the naive difference is 18.8 [-1.5, 39.0]. With no signal feed it is -10.8 [-32.1, 10.5].

2. **Looser cap adds risk, not free money: PASS.** Between caps 2 and 80, the seed sd of P&L goes 31.2 to 335.1, and the inventory term at cap 80 is 3.9 [-102.4, 110.1], which includes zero. Any rise in the mean comes from extra spread capture on more fills, shown in the cap table.

3. **Zero informed flow and zero fees is not presented as an edge: PASS.** P&L 337.6 [317.5, 357.7]. Verdict: not an edge: no informed flow and no fees, so the spread is paid by construction. Fair value still jumps here; only the takers who trade on it are gone.

## Why the old simulator showed +362 at cap 20

Replica of the review's maker on the same 20 seeds, delay 0. `spread` is capture against the pre-fill mid and `position` is inventory times mid change; they sum to P&L exactly.

| 5 | 129.3 [71.6, 187.1] | 123.8 | 773.2 [755.6, 790.9] | -643.9 [-699.8, -588.0] | 1339 | 2.6 |
| 10 | 226.1 [134.5, 317.6] | 196.3 | 798.1 [779.7, 816.5] | -572.1 [-661.8, -482.4] | 1380 | 5.2 |
| 20 | 362.1 [265.3, 459.0] | 207.6 | 814.8 [796.4, 833.2] | -452.7 [-545.4, -359.9] | 1407 | 10.2 |
| 40 | 509.8 [387.9, 631.8] | 261.4 | 825.9 [808.4, 843.3] | -316.0 [-441.8, -190.2] | 1425 | 15.5 |

Cap 20 minus cap 5, paired: P&L 232.8 [158.5, 307.1], of which position 191.2 [117.4, 265.0] (82%) and spread 41.6 [35.5, 47.6].

Variance ratio of the zero-intelligence mid (1 is a random walk), mean over seeds: 10 events 0.84, 100 events 0.74, 1000 events 0.61, 5000 events 0.23.

Reading: the zero-intelligence stream places limit orders relative to the opposite best, with no fair value, so its mid mean-reverts at long horizons, as the falling variance ratio shows. A maker is contrarian: it buys falls and sells rises. The position term is the cost of holding that inventory, and a wider cap shrinks the loss because a bigger contrarian book is paid by the reversion instead of being forced out at the cap. That looks like a property of the generator, not of the strategy. On the martingale fair value above, the same looser cap shows no such gain in the inventory term. This evidence is an attribution and a variance ratio, not a controlled removal of the reversion from the old stream; the later controls in results_control.md are inconclusive, so reversion is only partly supported as the cause. The old replay also never reacts to the injected orders, which is not separately measured here.

## Assumptions that drive these results

- Fair value: compound Poisson, 0.3/s, sd 1.5 ticks. Informed: 2 slots per jump, mean delay 20 ms, up to 5 lots.
- Agents get a free fair-value feed, delayed by their market-data latency. This creates the cancel race. The no-signal column shows what is left without it.
- The background book follows fair value with a 200 ms lag and is not competitive: nothing drives maker profit to zero except the optional fast rival, so positive maker P&L at base flow reflects the assumed share of uninformed flow.
- Hawkes market flow mu (0.8, 0.8), beta 3.0, sizes 1 to 3; it does not react to the book or to the agents.
- Latency is fixed with no jitter. No exchange throttles, no partial-size logic beyond the book, marks at final mid, no funding or capital cost.
- Parameters were set once by judgement and were not fitted to data. Harsh flow and no-signal runs are there to show how far the conclusions move.

Runtime 365 s with 8 workers.
